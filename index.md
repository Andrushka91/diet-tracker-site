# Diet Tracker

**Diet Tracker** is a free Windows 11 desktop application for planning your five daily meals (breakfast, morning snack, lunch, afternoon snack and dinner), tracking what you actually ate, attaching meal photographs and receiving native Windows reminders.

## Features

- Five customizable meal cards per day, with done / missed / pending tracking
- Meal templates, reminders and card colours
- Calendar and statistics of your progress
- Optional sign-in with Google to keep your data in your own Google Drive and use it on several PCs

## Your data and Google

Diet Tracker has no server. When you sign in with Google, the app stores your meal data only in a hidden, app-private folder of **your own** Google Drive (the `appDataFolder`, scope `drive.appdata`). The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
