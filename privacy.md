# Diet Tracker privacy policy

*Version 1.0 - effective 29 October 2026. Applies to Diet Tracker for Windows.*

## Summary

Diet Tracker keeps your meal plan, your completion history and your photographs on your device and, when you sign in with Google, in a private application folder inside **your own** Google Drive. There is no Diet Tracker server: the application never sends your data to the developer.

## 1. Who processes your data

Diet Tracker is a local Windows application. The developer does not operate any backend service, does not receive your meal data and cannot read your cloud files.

Contact for privacy questions and deletion requests: andrushkadeveloper@gmail.com

## 2. Information the app processes

**Stored on your device:** meal plans (titles, descriptions, planned times), completion state and timestamps, attached photographs and thumbnails, templates, reminder settings and card colours, application preferences, synchronization bookkeeping and a technical log file that never contains credentials, tokens or meal content.

**Obtained from Google, only after you sign in:** a stable account identifier, your display name and your e-mail address. They are used only to show which account is connected and to keep accounts apart.

**Never collected:** advertising identifiers, location, contacts, health records, biometric data, other files on your device, or any file in your Google Drive outside the Diet Tracker application folder.

## 3. How the app uses Google user data

Diet Tracker requests these Google scopes: `openid`, `email`, `profile` and `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata`.

The `drive.appdata` scope lets the app read and write only its own hidden application folder (`appDataFolder`) in your Google Drive. The app uses it solely to store and synchronize your Diet Tracker data so you can use it on several Windows PCs or after reinstalling. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Google user data is used only to provide these features to you. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Diet Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 4. Storage and security

- All network traffic to Google uses HTTPS/TLS.
- The Google refresh token is encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows user account and never leaves your device.
- Cloud data is protected by Google's storage encryption and your account permissions. Diet Tracker does not add end-to-end encryption.

## 5. Your controls

- **Sign out** in Settings: pending changes are uploaded, the credentials are removed and the local working copy is deleted.
- **Export data** to a backup archive or a PDF report.
- **Revoke access** at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **Delete cloud data**: remove the Diet Tracker app data from Google Drive (Settings > Manage apps) or e-mail the contact address above.

## 6. Retention

Data is kept until you delete it. The local copy is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the app. Cloud data stays in your Google Drive until you delete it.

## 7. Children

Diet Tracker is a personal planning tool and is not directed at children. It contains no advertising or tracking.

## 8. Changes

Material changes to this policy are published on this page with a new version and date.
